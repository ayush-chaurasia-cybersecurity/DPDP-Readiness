    What this tool does

    Gives a structured, first-pass view of how prepared an organisation is for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025. It screens applicability, flags Significant Data Fiduciary indicators, and scores 38 controls across ten domains so teams can see priority gaps and decide where a deeper review is warranted.

    How it works

    The assessor classifies the organisation, then rates each applicable control from 0 (none) to 3 (operating and evidenced). Each control is weighted 1 to 3 by regulatory consequence, and the score is the weighted share of maximum points over answered, applicable controls. Any weight-3 control rated 0 caps the rating at Developing. Penalty tiers shown come from the Schedule to the Act. Processing happens locally in the browser.

    Accuracy and limitations

    Controls and references are drawn from the text of the Act and the Rules as notified on 13 Nov 2025. Weights and rating bands reflect professional judgement and have not been statistically validated or calibrated against enforcement outcomes. Results depend on self-reported inputs and are not verified against evidence. The tool does not cover sector regulation (for example RBI, IRDAI, SEBI, CERT-In), future government orders on cross-border transfer or localisation, or SDF notification decisions.
